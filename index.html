About

A New York-based real estate firm

Gemini Real Estate Associates is a New York-based real estate investment, development and advisory firm. We work across property types and the full life of a deal, from sourcing and underwriting through financing, execution and asset management.

Our approach is hands-on and relationship-driven. We have owned, operated and leased real estate ourselves, which shapes how we underwrite, who we partner with and how we advise.

Leadership

Shane Neuringer

Founder & Managing Principal

Shane has been involved in more than $3 billion of real estate transactions spanning acquisitions, development, financing and asset management. He has held senior positions at CB Developers, Stellar Management and Rockrose Development, all major New York investment and development companies, and served as an AVP in acquisitions and asset management at a $5 billion pension fund advisor.

He holds an M.S. in Real Estate Investment and Development from New York University and is a licensed New York State real estate broker.
